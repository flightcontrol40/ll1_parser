The Grammar uses the following form:
Productions must be in the form of 'Non-Terminal' ::= 'Production'
Epsilon is represented by a lowercase 'e'
The right hand side of the production may contain: Non-terminals, Terminals, or epsilon
Spaces can be used to separate symbols in the right hand side of a production The '|' symbol can be used in the right hand side of the production as a short hand.
Ie:
D ::= B | C is short-hand for
D ::= B
D ::= C
